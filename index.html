Questions

Is it safe to give my API key?

Create a key with read-only rights. Buy&Sell checks the rights when you connect it and refuses keys that can trade or withdraw. The key is stored encrypted with your journal.

Which exchanges are supported?

OKX, Bybit (including Bybit EU) and Hyperliquid today; Binance is next. Several exchanges can share one journal.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Open the site on your phone and add it to the home screen — it works like an app. Desktop and phone share one journal.

Can you see my trades?

No. The journal is encrypted on your device; our server keeps only encrypted files and cannot read them.

How do I pay and cancel?

Plans are paid in USDT for a month or a year; nothing renews automatically. Upgrade any time — the unused days of your current plan are kept. When the paid period ends, trades keep loading and are saved, but only the trade list stays open until you renew; you can export your data any time.

Is this investment advice?

No. Buy&Sell shows facts from your own trades and the market. Decisions are yours; trading carries risk of loss.

Does it help with taxes?

The tax report shows each closed trade in your country's currency at the official rate on the close date, with yearly totals and CSV. It is not tax advice.
